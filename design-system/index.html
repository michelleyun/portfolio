Principles

The rules every page follows.

Dots are the signature

The name, the photos and the case preview art are printed in halftone. Nothing else is.

Color is earned

Black and white by default. Color lives in the photos and in one dusty tone per case study.

Plain language

Every visual reads clearly to someone outside ad tech. No jargon, no filler captions.

Calm motion

Short fades, never full-screen transitions. Reduce Motion skips the intro and stills the page.

Even rhythm

The same spacing between every section. Columns line up at the top and bottom.

Pills for actions

Every button, tag and nav link is fully rounded, in the body face.

Works in the dark

Every color is defined for light and dark. KJ inverts to stay visible.

Phone first

Columns stack, menus collapse to a Menu pill, tables drop their least important column.
